### Resolved — store operations are serialized and destructive controls are locked

`createChartHistoryStore()` now maintains one promise queue and sends reads, saves, targeted deletes, and clears through it (`apps/web/src/history/chartHistoryStore.ts:337-438`). Each queued read/modify/write observes the preceding operation's durable result. A failed operation is normalized and cannot poison later queue work. This prevents same-store save/save loss, delete/delete resurrection, and stale save/delete/clear projections.

Deferred-promise race tests prove two saves retain both entries, two deletes remove both, save-before-delete retains only the intended new entry, and clear-after-delete remains cleared (`apps/web/src/history/chartHistoryStore.test.ts:160-238`). The assertions verify that the second operation does not open a database session before the first write completes and verify the final durable singleton, not only returned statuses.

The history panel now disables every entry Delete button and Clear while either destructive operation is pending (`apps/web/src/history/ChartHistoryPanel.tsx:217-235`). Component tests hold deletion and clear promises open and verify all destructive controls remain locked until completion (`apps/web/src/history/ChartHistoryPanel.test.tsx:178-236`). Denied operations still retain visible data and become retryable after the failure result.
