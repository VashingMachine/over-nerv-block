## Generator and contract verification

- The owned quality fixture generates exact note times: Easy `[1,3,5,7]`, Medium `[1,2,3,4,5,6,7]`, and Hard every half second from 1 through 7 seconds.
- Golden replay with the same analysis and seed is deeply equal. Generated IDs canonically cover the complete accepted analysis, generation rules, seed, difficulty, and selected note content without including a filename.
- Every generated chart records schema version, `quality-dsp-v1`, `difficulty-generator-v1`, contract version, difficulty, seed, counts, spacing/density rules, and low-confidence-guard status.
- Table-driven checks cover intro/outro bounds, strict ordering, uniqueness through spacing, one lane, density, shared playable-schema acceptance, truthful downbeat/beat/onset sources, nested Easy ⊆ Medium ⊆ Hard notes, and whole-track coverage across 60–240 BPM and certain/uncertain meters.
- Adversarial spacing checks prove that a strong middle beat cannot falsely reject two feasible outer anchors and that a density-limited chart reaches the song tail instead of filling only its prefix.
- Low-confidence tests exclude weak beats without breaking nesting. Uncertain meter uses deterministic four-beat anchors. Sparse/short input produces `insufficient_safe_beats`.
- Negative schema cases reject wrong analyzer/generator versions, fractional seeds, mismatched difficulty rules, intro notes, and manual notes.
